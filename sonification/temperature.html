What am I hearing?

Each soft tone represents one annual average. Warmer years produce higher pitches; cooler years produce lower pitches. The glowing point follows the year you are hearing. Pitch represents temperature, while volume stays consistent.

What do these values mean?

These are temperature anomalies: differences from the 1951–1980 average, measured in °C. They are not absolute temperatures. We use the annual J–D column from your NASA dataset.
