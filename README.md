🚀 About the Project

EARTH JUKE is an interactive web-based project developed for the NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026 under the challenge The Earth Information Jukebox.

Our project aims to make Earth-system information more engaging and easier to explore through an interactive dashboard experience.

EARTH JUKE brings together four important environmental themes, allowing users to explore different aspects of our changing planet through visual data representations and interactive features.

🎯 Problem Statement

Earth-system information is often complex and difficult for people to understand.

Environmental changes such as rising temperatures, melting Arctic sea ice, forest cover changes and increasing atmospheric carbon dioxide are important, but presenting this information in an engaging and accessible way remains a challenge.

EARTH JUKE addresses this by bringing different environmental themes together in one interactive platform.

💡 Our Solution

We developed EARTH JUKE, an interactive Earth Information Jukebox featuring four major dashboards.

Users can explore different environmental categories and understand various aspects of Earth's changing systems through visual representations.

Our project introduces a sound experience in the Temperature dashboard.

🌎 Four Main Dashboards

1. Cryosphere – Arctic Sea Ice

Explores changes in Arctic sea ice and the frozen regions of our planet.

2. Earth – Temperature

Presents temperature-related information and visualizations to explore changes over time.

Special Feature: Sound effects are currently implemented in this dashboard.

3. Biosphere – Forest Cover

Explores forest cover and changes in Earth's green landscapes.

4. Atmosphere – Carbon Dioxide

Focuses on atmospheric carbon dioxide and its environmental significance.

🔊 Sound Experience

One of the interesting features of EARTH JUKE is its sound experience.

Currently:

- Temperature dashboard – Sound effects implemented.
- Cryosphere dashboard – Sound effects not yet implemented.
- Biosphere dashboard – Sound effects not yet implemented.
- Atmosphere dashboard – Sound effects not yet implemented.

Extending the sound experience to the remaining dashboards is part of our future scope.
